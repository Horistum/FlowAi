# v0.9.5.7.2 Governance Scanner Honesty

## Summary

v0.9.5.7.2 removes source-word obfuscation and changes governance from raw text matching to structural mechanism validation.

## Added

- `KotlinSourceBoundaryScanner`
- `FlowGovernanceScannerHonestyTests`
- structural source-scan coverage for comments, strings and real forbidden symbols
- structured semantic mechanism validation
- structured projection mechanism validation

## Removed

- character-code word builder from target renderers
- character-code and concatenation workarounds for secret binding and blocked parameter vocabulary
- description-wide forbidden word scanning in semantic and projection validators

## Governance result

Forbidden architecture catalog entries are matched as Kotlin symbols outside comments and literals. Target renderer diagnostics and target-native secret binding vocabulary remain readable.

Semantic and projection gates reject raw runtime ownership through declaration and field structure rather than ordinary explanatory language.
